Turn 4 · 3a sharpened · honest press

Same key, pushed down

Travel comes from geometry, not from moving the label. Every key is a face plate on a dark plinth: at rest the face sits at the top of its box and the plinth shows as a hard edge underneath; pressed, the face drops to the bottom of that same box and the plinth vanishes. Hit box never changes size, gradient never flips. Radii tightened. 4a 1px radius, 4b square, 4c deep-set.
