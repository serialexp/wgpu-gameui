What changed

Each group of 8 owns its label — click one to rename it, Enter commits, Escape cancels. Repeated names are just the current teardown convention, so nothing groups rows together behind your back.

Selection can't use the accent colour here — it would lie about the swatch underneath. It's a black-then-white double ring, which stays legible on any of the 255.

Unused index 255 is hatched rather than left black, so it isn't mistaken for a reserved colour. Reserved rows keep their labels dimmed and their cells hairline-separated so the run of black still reads as 64 slots.

The readout follows the pointer and falls back to the selection, so you can survey indices without losing your place.
