Toolbar

A strip of press keys that docks to any edge. The key — face plate on a plinth, with real 2px travel — is the core widget of the whole system; everything clickable in the chrome is a variation on it.

Key states
The press key is the core widget of the system: a face plate on a plinth. The plinth carries 2px of bottom padding; pressing moves it to the top so the face drops into its own shadow without the footprint shifting. Do not substitute a transform.

Docked left
Default orientation. Group separators run across the short axis; the drag grip sits at the leading end. The latched key here is Move.

Docked top
The same strip rotated: flex direction, separator orientation, edge shadow and tooltip side all flip together. Four edges are supported — top, right, bottom, left.

Tooltip
No delay, no fade. Offset 7px on the strip's outward side, with the shortcut set in mono at a lower contrast than the label.

Dock popover
Opened by the ⋯ overflow key, the drag grip, or a right-click anywhere on the strip. Same sheet style as a menu, narrowed to 142px, with a mono uppercase title row and a radio dot on the current edge.
