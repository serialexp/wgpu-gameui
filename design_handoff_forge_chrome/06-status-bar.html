Status bar

The quietest surface in the app, and the only place a completed action is reported back.

Status bar
26px, entirely mono. Left: dock toggles, then the echo of the last action taken from any menu or tool. Right: current snap, shading and gizmo state, separated by 1px dividers. Every menu activation writes its own label into the echo — that is the whole feedback loop for menu commands.
◧ ▤ ◨ last:Bake Lightmaps snap 1 m shading Lit gizmos on
Dock toggle states
The same latched treatment as a toolbar key, at 18px: an open panel keeps its toggle pressed and accented. Off and hover are unlit.
